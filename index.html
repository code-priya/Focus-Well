🔍 Key Insights

  • Study vs. Sleep: High stress levels are strongly correlated with longer study hours and shorter sleep duration.
  • Balance is Key: Moderate stress levels typically reflect a balance between academics and physical/social activities.
  • Academic Success: The average GPA suggests a positive relationship between balanced lifestyle habits and performance.
