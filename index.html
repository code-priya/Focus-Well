📊 Student Lifestyle & Stress Analysis

Exploring how lifestyle habits affect student stress and academic performance

🎯 Dataset Objective

This dataset explores how lifestyle patterns influence student stress levels and academic performance. It includes lifestyle attributes such as study hours, sleep, social activities, physical activity and GPA.

📋 Dataset Features

  • 🕒 Study Hours Per Day
  • 😴 Sleep Hours Per Day
  • 🏃 Physical Activity Hours
  • 🗣 Social Hours
  • 🎭 Extracurricular Hours
  • 🎓 GPA
  • ⚡ Stress Level (Low / Moderate / High)

📊 Dataset Statistics

Records

2000

Features

8 Columns

Target Variable

Stress Level

📈 Stress Level Distribution

📉 Key Insight

⚡ High stress is associated with longer study hours and less sleep.

⚖ Moderate stress shows balanced lifestyle patterns.

😊 Low stress correlates with higher physical activity and social engagement.
